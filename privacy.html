Part A

Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Transition Properties LLC ("we," "us," "our," or "Transition Properties") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect information when you visit transitionfl.com (the "Site"), submit information through our forms, or communicate with us by phone, SMS, or email.

By using the Site or providing your information to us, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

2. Who We Are

Business Name: Transition Properties LLC
State of Formation: Florida
Mailing Address: Cape Coral, FL
Email: info@transitionfl.com
Phone: (239) 766-6978
Website: transitionfl.com

3. Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you when you provide it, automatically when you visit the Site, and from third parties (such as public records databases) for our property research activities.

Information you provide directly

  • Name
  • Phone number(s)
  • Email address
  • Mailing or property address
  • Property details and condition
  • Reason for selling, timeline, and financial situation (only what you choose to share)
  • Communications you send us (call recordings, text messages, emails, form submissions)

Information collected automatically

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type and operating system
  • Pages viewed and time on Site
  • Referring URL
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies (see Part C below)

Information from third parties (for property research only)

  • Public records data (ownership, tax assessments, mortgage liens, foreclosure filings)
  • Skip-traced contact information from licensed data providers
  • Property valuation data

4. How We Use Your Information

We use information to:

  • Respond to inquiries you submit through the Site
  • Evaluate properties for potential purchase
  • Send communications related to property purchase offers, scheduling, transactions, and follow-up
  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Site
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Protect against fraud, abuse, or illegal activity
  • Maintain records of consent for telephone and SMS communications

5. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal information based on:

  • Your consent (which you may withdraw at any time)
  • Performance of a contract or pre-contractual steps at your request
  • Compliance with legal obligations
  • Our legitimate business interests in operating a real estate purchasing business, balanced against your rights

6. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only as follows:

  • Service Providers. Third parties that help us operate (hosting, CRM, telephone/SMS infrastructure, accounting, legal). These providers are contractually obligated to protect your information.
  • Title Companies, Closing Agents, and Attorneys. When transacting on a property, with your consent.
  • Investors and Buyers. If you enter into a purchase contract, we may assign that contract to an end buyer (see Real Estate Disclaimer). We share only what is necessary to complete the transaction.
  • Legal Compliance. When required by law, court order, or to protect our rights or others' safety.
  • Business Transfer. If Transition Properties is sold or merged, your information may transfer to the successor entity.
SMS opt-in data and consent records are never shared, sold, or transferred outside Transition Properties for any purpose. See Part B, section 8 below.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Communication records (calls, texts, emails) are retained for a minimum of seven (7) years for tax, accounting, and legal compliance purposes.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Opt out of future communications
  • Withdraw consent

To exercise any right, email info@transitionfl.com with your request. We will respond within 30 days.

Florida residents: You have rights under the Florida Digital Bill of Rights including rights to access, correct, delete, and opt out of processing of personal information.

9. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18.

10. Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. You provide information at your own risk.

11. Third-Party Links

The Site may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be posted prominently on the Site.

Part B

SMS Terms & Consent

1. SMS Program Overview

Transition Properties LLC operates an SMS communications program for individuals who have opted in to receive text messages regarding the potential purchase of their real property. This section governs that program and is the dedicated reference for any SMS-related compliance question.

2. Consent

Transition Properties may send SMS/text messages to individuals who have provided prior express written consent through one of the following methods: (1) submitting a contact form on transitionfl.com, (2) providing verbal consent during a phone conversation, or (3) replying affirmatively to an outreach message.

Consent is not a condition of any purchase.

3. Message Types & Frequency

You may receive messages including:

  • Follow-up regarding your property inquiry
  • Scheduling and confirmation of property walkthroughs
  • Cash offer details and updates
  • Purchase agreement and closing coordination
  • Transaction status updates

Message frequency varies based on engagement, typically 1–5 messages per week during active conversations and reduces significantly outside of active engagement periods.

4. Message and Data Rates

Standard message and data rates may apply based on your mobile carrier plan. Transition Properties does not charge for any messages we send.

5. Opt-Out (STOP)

To stop receiving SMS messages, reply STOP to any message. You will receive a one-time confirmation that you have been unsubscribed. After opting out, you will receive no further messages unless you re-subscribe by replying START or providing fresh consent.

You may also opt out by:

  • Calling (239) 766-6978
  • Emailing info@transitionfl.com
  • Asking to be removed during any phone conversation

6. Help (HELP)

For assistance, reply HELP to any message. You will receive contact information for our support. You may also contact us at info@transitionfl.com or (239) 766-6978.

7. Carriers & Eligibility

This program is supported on major U.S. carriers including AT&T, T-Mobile, Verizon, U.S. Cellular, Boost, MetroPCS, and others. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

You must be 18 or older and the legal owner (or authorized representative of the owner) of the property being discussed to participate. Transition Properties reserves the right to modify or terminate this SMS program at any time without notice.

8. No Sharing of SMS Data

All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties for any purpose, including marketing or promotional purposes. This includes affiliates, vendors, and partners. SMS opt-in data and consent records are never shared, sold, or transferred outside Transition Properties.
Part C

Cookie Policy

1. What Cookies We Use

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site function, remember your preferences, and provide analytics.

Strictly Necessary Cookies

Required for the Site to function (e.g., session management, security). Cannot be disabled.

Analytics Cookies

Help us understand how visitors use the Site. These cookies collect aggregated, anonymized data such as page views, time on site, and traffic sources.

Functional Cookies

Remember your preferences (e.g., form data) to enhance your experience.

Marketing Cookies

Where used, marketing cookies help us understand the effectiveness of our outreach. We will update this section if and when we deploy advertising pixels.

2. Managing Cookies

Most browsers allow you to:

  • Block all cookies
  • Delete existing cookies
  • Set preferences for specific sites

Note: disabling necessary cookies may impair Site functionality. Browser-specific instructions:

  • Chrome: chrome://settings/cookies
  • Firefox: about:preferences#privacy
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

3. Do Not Track

Our Site does not currently respond to "Do Not Track" browser signals. You may opt out of analytics by adjusting browser settings or using opt-out tools provided by analytics services.
